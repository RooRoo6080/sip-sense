# SipSense: stay hydrated
Created using Flutter.

---

It is estimated that 75% of Americans consume 2.5 cups of water a day of water a day—much less than the 8.5 cups recommended. Hydration is a fundamental necessity of the human body, and being severely dehydrated can cause a multitude of health issues, such as dizziness, concentration difficulties, headaches, and more. Furthermore, dehydration causes between 700,000 and 800,000 pediatric deaths worldwide, as well as half a million hospitalizations in the US alone. Severe dehydration spanning three-quarters of the American population is a concern we shouldn't ignore, especially since water is simply an arm's length away in a water bottle. That's why I created SipSense.

SipSense is a smart hydration assistant: an app and device combo that tracks how much water is consumed, sends reminders when the user is dehydrated, and graphs this information to review if the daily goals have been met. 
First, the user inputs their weight to automatically create an editable consumption goal. SipSense then connects to a Bluetooth device attached to the user's water bottle to communicate with it in order to track consumption. Whenever water is consumed, the device's flow meter lets the mobile application precisely understand the quantity consumed and the time. If water has not been consumed within a specified time interval, a reminder will be sent to the user’s phone. This data is tracked and presented on an analysis page with multiple graphs depicting actual consumption versus the user’s goal over various time periods. SipSense can also work without the Bluetooth helper device, letting you stay in control of your health with just a mobile app.

With SipSense, staying hydrated has never been easier - drink smarter and feel better!
